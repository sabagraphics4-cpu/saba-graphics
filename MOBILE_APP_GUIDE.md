# How to Install Saba Graphics Mobile App

## Your website is now a Progressive Web App (PWA)!

This means visitors can install it on their phones like a real app, and it will work offline.

## Installation Instructions:

### For Android (Chrome):
1. Open the website on your Android phone in Chrome
2. Tap the menu (⋮) in the top right
3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
4. Confirm the installation
5. The Saba Graphics icon will appear on your home screen!

### For iPhone (Safari):
1. Open the website on your iPhone in Safari
2. Tap the Share button (□ with arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. The Saba Graphics icon will appear on your home screen!

### Features:
- ✅ Works offline
- ✅ Fast loading
- ✅ Home screen icon
- ✅ Full-screen experience
- ✅ No app store needed!

## Testing Locally:
For the app to be installable, you need to:
1. Use HTTPS (or localhost for testing)
2. Access the site from http://192.168.1.15:5000 on your phone (on same WiFi)
3. Or deploy to a live server with HTTPS

Note: PWAs work best on live servers with SSL certificates (HTTPS).
